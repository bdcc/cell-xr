Look through your screen

Your webcam tracks your eyes, and the picture is redrawn from exactly where you sit, so the monitor behaves like a window into a room behind it. Lean, duck and move closer to look around the targets.

Video never leaves your device; face tracking runs locally (MediaPipe). Calibrate the screen size once in the panel for true-to-scale depth.

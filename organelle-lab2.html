Organelle Lab

Closed, sliceable organelles grown from simple space-filling rules. Every membrane is a closed bag with a space inside, so a slice shows solid walls, like a textbook cross-section. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.

Slice
Nucleus

Rule: two membranes a fixed gap apart (the envelope is one thin closed bag), with pores where the two membranes fuse, spread evenly over the sphere.

Rough ER

Rule: generate strictly parallel cisternal sheets outwards from the envelope. We use pure 3D noise to carve meandering, maze-like gaps into the sheets, turning them into winding ribbons that double back without intersecting. Join sheets with twisted ramps, and fuse the inner layer directly to the nuclear envelope.

Smooth ER

Rule (coral growth): colonies of trunks start along the rough ER's outer edge, joined by a short web of tubes that blends into a holey base. All tips grow together along the curved surface of the outermost rough-ER shell: past its edge they stay in the shell's own surface (the sheet frays into tubes), and where they pass over the sheet they lift just clear of it, with a gentle wander. Tubes fork within that curved layer into two slightly thinner tubes (~0.9× the radius); growing tips are pushed sideways off nearby tubes, and a relaxation pass nudges crowded tubes apart (staying in the layer) so they bend round each other. With "Grow tubes to the slice", colonies start near the slice on the side you see, grow toward it, and end just past it, so the cut shows open tube ends like a textbook diagram. Tips that bump into another tube can fuse with it. Lace: tube ends join the nearest other tube, and neighbouring tubes link sideways, closing loops with three-way junctions: the "reticulum" (net) of real smooth ER. Vesicles bud off the tubes, some on their way toward the Golgi. Real tubes are 50–100 nm across (radius 0.025–0.05 here, 1 unit ≈ 1 µm); the default is thicker so they're easy to see.

Golgi

Rule: stack curved closed discs (cisternae) at a fixed spacing; each disc is a little smaller and swollen at the rim; holes near the rims; vesicles bud off the edges.

Mitochondria

Rule: an outer capsule membrane; an inner membrane a fixed gap inside it; the inner membrane folds inward into plates (cristae) at a fixed spacing, alternating sides so the folds interleave and fill the space.

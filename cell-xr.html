Push and pull:

Window: the screen is a window into the scene. Push your hand toward the screen and the object moves away from you, deeper in; pull back to bring it closer. Mirror: the screen is a mirror. Push toward the screen and the object comes toward you, like your reflection. Turning and moving side to side work the same in both. Mirror is the default when the camera is on; change it any time here or in the panel.

Use your camera for hand tracking?

Hold N M K L O P to make a hand pose at the mouse pointer (poke, pinch, grab, thumb, open, fist), J for the finger gun. Drag to spin, Shift-drag to move, scroll for depth, B to explode, 1–4 to switch gesture sets. You can turn the camera on later from the panel.
